Android phones

Join the PayAviator test

For Android phones only. Thank you for helping us! It takes about 2 minutes. Just follow the 8 steps below.

  1. 1

    Join our testers group

    Tap the yellow button, then tap Join group.

    This is a free Google group. It lets Google know you are a tester. Use the same Gmail address that is on your Play Store.

    Join the testers group

    Example: tap Join group.

    Real screenshots: after you join, you will see PayAviator Testers in your groups.

  2. 2

    Use the right Gmail address

    Use the Gmail address we sent the invite to.

    It must be the same Google account that is signed in on your Play Store (the colorful triangle app). If you are not sure, open the Play Store and tap the circle at the top right to see your email.

    Example screen. Yours may look a little different.

  3. 3

    Open the test page

    After you join the group, tap Join the test in our email.

    Open the email on your Android phone, not on a computer. No email yet? Tap here to open the test page.

    Example screen. Yours may look a little different.

  4. 4

    Tap “Become a tester”

    Tap Become a tester.

    A Google page opens. It is safe. It is Google’s own page.

    Real screenshot. Tap inside the circle.

  5. 5

    Install the app

    Tap Download it on Google Play. Then tap Install.

    Wait a minute while it installs.

    Real screenshot. Tap inside the circle.

  6. 6

    Open PayAviator and sign in

    Tap Open. Then sign in with Google or with your email.

    Pick the way that is easiest for you.

    Real screenshot. Tap inside the circle.

  7. 7

    Stay in the test for 14 days

    Keep the app on your phone for 14 days.

    Google requires 14 days. It helps us get PayAviator into the Play Store. Please do not uninstall it. Opening it now and then helps too.

    Example screen. Yours may look a little different.

  8. 8

    Need help?

    Email support@payaviator.app

    Tell us what you see on your phone. We are happy to help.

Thank you! – PayAviator
