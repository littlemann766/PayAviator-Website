How it works & security

How Safe to Spend works

In PayAviator, Safe to Spend starts from Available now, the available balance of the checking and cash accounts you connect. It then keeps back a Payday Cushion you choose. Savings stay protected unless you include them.

Safe to Spend = Available now + Savings (only if included) − Cushion, never below $0

How connecting an account works

  1. You choose to connect (optional)
  2. Sign in to your PayAviator account
  3. Sign in to your bank through Plaid
  4. Account, balance & transaction info shown in PayAviator
  5. Disconnect any time

Connected financial accounts

Tap to read more

PayAviator uses Plaid to let users connect supported financial institutions. When a user chooses to connect an account, PayAviator may access account and balance information and transaction information made available through Plaid so the app can display financial activity and support budgeting and money-management features.

PayAviator does not ask users to provide their online-banking password directly to PayAviator. Account connections are initiated through Plaid. Users control whether they connect an account and can disconnect connected accounts.

Connecting an account requires a PayAviator account (sign in with Google or email). Bills you add stay on your phone. Connected bank data (synced transactions and connection details) is stored on PayAviator's servers so it can sync. Plaid access tokens are encrypted there and never sent to your phone, and deleting your PayAviator account removes your bank connections at Plaid and deletes that stored data.

For more information, read the PayAviator Privacy Policy.
