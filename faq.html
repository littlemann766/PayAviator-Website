No. Connecting a financial account is optional. You can enter bills, income, budgets and debts yourself without a PayAviator account. An account (Google or email sign-in) is only needed to connect a bank, and you can disconnect connected accounts at any time.

Safe to Spend starts from Available now, the available balance of the checking and cash accounts you include. It then subtracts your Payday Cushion, which is a percentage of your most recent paycheck (20% by default) or a dollar amount you set. Savings accounts are protected by default; turn on "Include savings in Safe to Spend" to count them. The result never goes below $0. Without connected accounts, Available now comes from PayAviator's own records of checks and funds received, minus paid bills, manual spending and savings.

PayAviator does not ask you to provide your online-banking password directly to PayAviator. Account connections are initiated through Plaid, and authentication with supported institutions is handled through the Plaid connection experience.

Your first bank account is on us, and bills, budgets and the other tools are free. To link more accounts, get a Plus subscription: $3.99 a month or $29.99 a year (save 37%). It renews automatically until you cancel. See Pricing and the Terms. PayAviator is for adults 18 and older.

In PayAviator's Payoff Planner you look at one card at a time and choose how to pay it. Snowball pays the card's minimum payment (if you haven't saved one, PayAviator uses 3% of the balance, at least $25). You can add an extra monthly payment: it goes to your smallest balance first, and when that balance is paid off, its payment rolls over to the next one. Snowball shows the projected payoff date, the number of months and the projected interest. Avalanche shows paying the card's full balance off, including the month's interest. Both show how much of the card's credit limit is used now and after the payment. These are estimates, not financial advice. Read more.
