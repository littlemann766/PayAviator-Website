Bills & Calendar

Organize recurring bills, due dates, payments and income in one place.

More details
  • Keep recurring bills and due dates organized
  • Track which payments are made
  • See income and bills together on a calendar

Budget & Spending

Track monthly spending and compare activity with the limits you set.

More details
  • Set monthly spending limits
  • Track spending through the month
  • Compare activity against your limits

Payoff Planner

Pick a card and preview its payoff with Snowball or Avalanche.

More details
  • Add a card with its balance, credit limit, APR and minimum payment
  • Snowball: pay the minimum, add an optional extra monthly payment, and see the payoff date, months and projected interest
  • Avalanche: pay the full balance off
  • Gauge shows how much of the card's credit is used, and how much after the payment

Safe-to-Spend View

See what you can spend right now: what's available, minus the Payday Cushion you choose.

More details
  • Starts from Available now: your connected checking and cash balances
  • Keeps a Payday Cushion: a percentage of your paycheck or a set dollar amount
  • Savings stay protected unless you include them

Income & Hours

Keep track of paycheck information and work hours alongside your bills.

More details
  • Record paycheck information
  • Log the hours you work
  • See income next to upcoming bills
