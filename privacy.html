Pay-Pilot Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026

Pay-Pilot is a personal finance application designed to help users organize bills, budgets, spending, income, debts, and connected financial-account information.

Information you provide

Pay-Pilot may process information you enter into the app, such as bills, income, spending, budgets, debt information, savings settings, notes, reminders, and other information used to provide app features.

Connected financial accounts

If you choose to connect a financial institution, Pay-Pilot uses Plaid to facilitate that connection. Depending on the features you authorize and the data made available by your institution, Pay-Pilot may receive account information, balances, and transaction information. This information is used to provide user-requested personal-finance features such as displaying account activity, supporting budgeting, and helping calculate financial summaries.

Pay-Pilot does not require you to give your online-banking password directly to Pay-Pilot. Authentication with supported institutions is handled through the Plaid connection experience.

How information is used

  • Provide and operate Pay-Pilot features.
  • Display and organize financial information requested by the user.
  • Support budgeting, spending, bill-management, and financial-summary features.
  • Maintain and improve reliability and security.

Sharing

Pay-Pilot does not sell users' personal financial information. Information may be processed by service providers used to operate app functionality, such as Plaid for connected financial accounts, subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

User choices

Connecting a financial account is optional. Users may disconnect accounts and may stop using connected-account features. Users should use the available account-management controls for their connected institution or Plaid connection when they want to revoke access.

Security

Pay-Pilot uses reasonable safeguards intended to protect information. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children

Pay-Pilot is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Pay-Pilot changes. The effective date above will be updated when material revisions are published.
