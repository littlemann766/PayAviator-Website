PayAviator Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026 · Policy version: 2026-10f

In short

  • If you use PayAviator without signing in, your bills, income, spending and budgets stay on your device. If you sign in and use cloud sync, copies are also stored on our servers (api.payaviator.app, hosted on Railway with a Postgres database) so they can sync between your phone and the web.
  • Bank data comes through Plaid; we never see your bank password.
  • Our servers identify you by a user ID; your email is used for sign-in and receipts.
  • We don't sell or share your personal information.
  • Your first bank, with all its accounts, is on us; a Plus subscription links more banks.
  • PayAviator is for adults 18 and older.
  • You can delete your account any time.

PayAviator is a personal finance application designed to help users organize bills, budgets, spending, income, debts, and connected financial-account information.

Information you provide

PayAviator may process information you enter into the app, such as bills, income, spending, budgets, debt information, savings settings, notes, reminders, and other information used to provide app features.

Account and sign-in

You can use PayAviator's bill, income, spending, and budgeting features without a PayAviator account. An account is needed only to connect a financial institution and sync connected-account data.

PayAviator accounts use Firebase Authentication, a Google service. You can sign in with your Google account or with an email address and password. Firebase Authentication stores a unique user ID for your account, your email address, and basic sign-in records such as when the account was created and when you last signed in. If you sign in with Google, Firebase also receives your Google account display name and a link to your profile photo. If you use an email address and password, your password is handled by Firebase Authentication; PayAviator's servers never receive or store it.

PayAviator's servers identify you by your Firebase user ID only. They do not store your email address, name, or photo.

Connected financial accounts

If you choose to connect a financial institution, PayAviator uses Plaid to facilitate that connection. Depending on the features you authorize and the data made available by your institution, PayAviator may receive account information, balances, and transaction information. This information is used to provide user-requested personal-finance features such as displaying account activity, supporting budgeting, and helping calculate financial summaries.

PayAviator does not require you to give your online-banking password directly to PayAviator. Authentication with supported institutions is handled through the Plaid connection experience.

Connecting a financial institution requires a PayAviator account. When you connect, Plaid provides PayAviator's servers with an access token for that connection. Access tokens are encrypted on PayAviator's servers (AES-256) and are never sent to your phone.

Plaid also notifies PayAviator's servers when a connection has new or updated transactions, when new accounts are available, or when a connection needs attention, for example when you need to sign in to your bank again, a connection is about to expire, or access was revoked. PayAviator uses these notifications to sync transactions and to show a Reconnect option in the app when a connection needs to be updated.

Before Plaid opens, PayAviator shows a short notice explaining what connecting means, with links to this policy and Plaid's privacy policy. When you tap Continue, the app records on your phone that you agreed, including the time, the policy version, whether you were connecting or reconnecting, and the app version. If you choose Not now, no connection is started.

When you use Refresh to check a live bank balance, we keep a short log of that request (your user ID, the bank connection ID, the time, and whether it succeeded or failed) so we can reconcile billing from our data provider. This log contains no balances, account numbers or bank credentials, and it may be kept after you delete your account.

Banks connected with app versions released before sign-in was added need to be connected again after you sign in. After the sign-in update is released, PayAviator will remove those earlier bank connections at Plaid and delete their stored data.

What's stored where

  • On your phone: bills, income, spending, budgets, reminders, and other information you enter are stored on your phone. If you don't sign in or don't use cloud sync, this information stays on your phone and is not sent to our servers. While you're signed in, the app also keeps a copy of your connected-account and transaction information on your phone. That copy is removed when you sign out, when your sign-in session ends, when you delete your account, or when a different user signs in on the phone.
  • On PayAviator's servers: if you are signed in and use cloud sync, copies of your bills, income, spending, budgets and related settings you enter are stored in PayAviator's database on Railway (api.payaviator.app) and linked to your Firebase user ID, so they can sync between the app and payaviator.app. You can delete them by deleting your account. Connected bank data is also stored so it can sync. For each connected institution this includes the encrypted Plaid access token, the institution's name and identifier, and the connection's status, plus the transactions synced from Plaid and short-lived records used while a connection is being set up. These records are linked to your Firebase user ID. Account details and balances are not stored on PayAviator's servers; they are fetched live from Plaid when the app needs them. The servers also keep the one-way codes described in Preventing misuse of the free bank connection.
  • Device-lock preference: whether the optional app lock is on, and your reminder choices, stay on your phone only (see Optional device lock).
  • With Firebase Authentication: your sign-in account information, described in Account and sign-in.

PayAviator's servers also keep operational logs to troubleshoot problems and protect the service. These logs are limited to technical details such as your Firebase user ID, connection and request identifiers, error codes and messages, and counts. They do not include access tokens, passwords, or your transaction, account, or balance details. Logs are kept by Railway, PayAviator's hosting provider, for the period set by its plan.

Preventing misuse of the free bank connection

PayAviator includes one free connected bank (with all its accounts) per person. To stop the same bank account or phone from being used to claim more than one free connection, PayAviator's servers keep two one-way codes: one made from the bank and the last four digits of the connected account, and one made from an identifier for your phone. These codes are scrambled in a way that can't be turned back into your account number or phone identifier, and PayAviator never stores your full account number.

The codes are used only to check whether a free connection has already been used. They are not used for advertising, are not shared, and are not linked to your transactions. When you disconnect your free bank or delete your account, these codes are kept for 30 days so the free connection can't be reused right away, and then they are deleted.

Optional device lock

PayAviator can be locked with your phone's PIN, pattern, password, fingerprint or face scan. This is optional and is off unless you turn it on. After you connect a bank, the app suggests turning it on, with the choices Turn on lock and Not now. If it stays off while a bank is connected, the app may show a gentle reminder when you open it, at most about once every 7 days, with a Don't remind me option. You can turn the lock on or off at any time in Settings.

The lock uses your phone's own screen-lock and biometric system. PayAviator does not receive, see or store your fingerprint, face data, PIN or password. Whether the suggestion has been shown, when it was last shown, and whether you chose Don't remind me are saved only on your phone. They are not sent to PayAviator's servers, and they are cleared if you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Because the lock is optional, a phone that is unlocked or shared can open PayAviator and the bank information it shows while you are signed in; signing out removes bank data from your phone.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, PayAviator uses Firebase Cloud Messaging, a Google service, to send bill reminders and alerts such as when a bank connection needs to be reconnected. Your phone gets a push token from Firebase Cloud Messaging. If you are signed in, the app sends that token to PayAviator's servers, where it is stored with your Firebase user ID and the device platform so alerts reach your phone. Push tokens are used only to deliver PayAviator notifications. When you sign out, the app asks PayAviator's servers to remove that phone's token, and deleting your account removes all of your push tokens. Turning off notifications on your phone stops them from showing. Bill reminders scheduled on your phone do not need a push token.

Subscriptions and payments

Your first bank, with all its accounts, is on us. Linking more banks needs a PayAviator Plus subscription ($3.99 a month or $29.99 a year). In the Android app, subscriptions are sold through Google Play Billing. On payaviator.app, subscriptions are sold through Stripe. Google Play or Stripe processes your payment and handles your card or payment details; PayAviator never receives your full card number.

PayAviator receives only what it needs to turn Plus on for your account: your Firebase user ID, the plan, the subscription status and renewal or end date, and a purchase or customer reference from Google Play or Stripe. If you subscribe on the web, Stripe also receives your email address so it can send receipts. Google Play and Stripe keep their own payment and purchase records under their own privacy policies.

How information is used

  • Provide and operate PayAviator features.
  • Display and organize financial information requested by the user.
  • Support budgeting, spending, bill-management, and financial-summary features.
  • Authenticate your PayAviator account and keep connected-account requests tied to your account.
  • Sync connected-account data and keep bank connections working.
  • Maintain and improve reliability and security.
  • Prevent misuse of the free bank connection.
  • Send bill reminders and connection alerts you allow.
  • Check and manage your Plus subscription.

Sharing

PayAviator does not sell your personal information and does not share it with third parties for their own purposes, including advertising. PayAviator shows no ads and does not use an advertising ID. Information is processed only by the service providers listed below to operate the app, under their own terms and privacy practices, or when required by law.

Service providers

PayAviator uses these service providers to operate the app. Each processes information under its own terms and privacy practices.

  • Plaid connects your financial institutions and provides account, balance, and transaction information.
  • Firebase Authentication (Google) provides sign-in and stores your sign-in account information.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) delivers push notifications using your device's push token.
  • Google Play Billing processes Plus subscriptions bought in the Android app.
  • Stripe processes Plus subscriptions bought on payaviator.app.
  • Railway hosts PayAviator's servers, the database that stores connected bank data, and the servers' operational logs.
  • Cloudflare provides DNS and protection for payaviator.app and api.payaviator.app. Traffic to those addresses passes through Cloudflare, which can see technical request details such as your IP address and the pages requested, and the encrypted content in transit. We do not use it to read your financial data.
  • Zoho Mail provides PayAviator's support email (support@payaviator.app). It sees the messages you send us and our replies, including your email address and anything you choose to include. Please don't email bank passwords or full account numbers.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the payaviator.app website pages. GitHub can see ordinary web request details such as your IP address when you load the site. It does not receive your bills, budgets, or bank data.
  • Google Play (Play Integrity) provides device integrity checks used to prevent misuse of the free bank connection.

User choices

Connecting a financial account is optional. Users may disconnect accounts and may stop using connected-account features. Users should use the available account-management controls for their connected institution or Plaid connection when they want to revoke access.

Signing in is optional unless you want to connect a financial institution. You can sign out at any time. Signing out removes bank data from your phone; the bills and other information you entered stay. Disconnecting a bank in the app (Settings > Connected Accounts) removes that connection at Plaid and deletes its transactions stored on PayAviator's servers.

Deleting your account

You can delete your PayAviator account in the app under Settings > Connected Accounts, or request deletion by email as described on the Delete your account page. For your security, if you haven't signed in recently, you'll be asked to sign in again first. Deleting your account removes every bank connection at Plaid and then deletes all of your data stored on PayAviator's servers: your bank connections, synced transactions, pending connection requests, connection-token records, and push tokens. If you use cloud sync, the copies of your bills, income, spending, cushion and budget stored on PayAviator's servers are deleted with your account too, whether you signed in on the app or on the website. The one-way codes used to prevent misuse of the free bank connection are deleted 30 days later. After that, your sign-in account is deleted from Firebase Authentication.

If Plaid can't remove one of your bank connections, that bank's connection and transactions stay on PayAviator's servers so the removal can be tried again, and your sign-in account is not deleted yet. Banks that were removed stay removed. The app tells you the deletion didn't finish, and you can try again.

Bills, income, spending, and other information you added stay on your phone after you delete your account. You can delete that information in the app or by uninstalling the app. Uninstalling doesn't delete backup or export files you saved yourself, wherever you chose to save them; delete those separately if you no longer want them.

Security

All data sent between the app, payaviator.app, and PayAviator's servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Every request for connected-account data is authenticated with your Firebase ID token, and PayAviator's servers return only the data linked to your account. Plaid access tokens are encrypted at rest (AES-256) and are never sent to your phone. Notifications from Plaid are accepted only when Plaid's signature is valid.

You can add an optional device lock (see Optional device lock) for extra protection on your phone. PayAviator uses reasonable safeguards intended to protect information. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Age requirement

PayAviator is intended only for adults 18 and older. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us information, email support@payaviator.app and we will delete it.

Changes

This policy may be updated as PayAviator changes. The effective date above will be updated when material revisions are published.

Contact

Privacy, data, or support questions, and account or data deletion requests, can be sent to support@payaviator.app.
