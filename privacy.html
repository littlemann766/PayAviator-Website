PayAviator Privacy Policy

Version: 2026-11a · Effective date: November 9, 2026. This replaces version 2026-10g, effective October 17, 2026. The archived text is at privacy version 2026-10g.

The Android app stores a policy version on the phone when you continue a bank connection. An install that still stores 2026-10g consented to that archived version. That includes the Play closed-test build 4.2.6 (version code 420600), which stays the active closed-test build while a newer build is in review. Opening this page does not change the version stored on that phone. A later app update records 2026-11a only after that update is installed and you continue a connection under it.

In short

  • If you use PayAviator without signing in, your bills, income, spending and budgets stay on your device. If you sign in and use cloud sync, copies are also stored on our servers (api.payaviator.app, hosted on Railway with a Postgres database) so they can sync between your phone and the web.
  • Bank data comes through Plaid; we never see your bank password.
  • Our servers identify you by a user ID; your email is used for sign-in and receipts.
  • We don't sell your personal information or share it for advertising or for other companies' own purposes. The service providers that run PayAviator are listed below.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel Plus. A few records, listed below, are not erased immediately.
  • Your first bank, with all its accounts, is on us; a Plus subscription links more banks.
  • PayAviator is for adults 18 and older.
  • You can delete your account with one confirmation. Disconnecting one bank connection is separate: it keeps your PayAviator account and does not close the account at your bank.

PayAviator is a personal finance application designed to help users organize bills, budgets, spending, income, debts, and connected financial-account information.

Information you provide

PayAviator may process information you enter into the app, such as bills, income, spending, budgets, debt information, savings settings, notes, reminders, and other information used to provide app features.

Account and sign-in

You can use PayAviator's bill, income, spending, and budgeting features without a PayAviator account. An account is needed only to connect a financial institution and sync connected-account data.

PayAviator accounts use Firebase Authentication, a Google service. You can sign in with your Google account or with an email address and password. Firebase Authentication stores a unique user ID for your account, your email address, and basic sign-in records such as when the account was created and when you last signed in. If you sign in with Google, Firebase also receives your Google account display name and a link to your profile photo. If you use an email address and password, your password is handled by Firebase Authentication; PayAviator's servers never receive or store it.

PayAviator database rows for an account are keyed by your Firebase user ID. Those rows do not include your email address, name, or photo. Firebase Authentication, a Google service, does store the email and, for Google sign-in, the display name and profile-photo link. If web checkout creates a Stripe customer, Stripe receives the email from the sign-in token. PayAviator then stores the Stripe customer id with the user id. It does not receive the full card number.

Connected financial accounts

If you choose to connect a financial institution, PayAviator uses Plaid to facilitate that connection. Depending on the features you authorize and the data made available by your institution, PayAviator may receive account information, balances, and transaction information. This information is used to provide user-requested personal-finance features such as displaying account activity, supporting budgeting, and helping calculate financial summaries.

PayAviator does not require you to give your online-banking password directly to PayAviator. Authentication with supported institutions is handled through the Plaid connection experience.

Connecting a financial institution requires a PayAviator account. When you connect, Plaid provides PayAviator's servers with an access token for that connection. Access tokens are encrypted on PayAviator's servers (AES-256) and are never sent to your phone.

Plaid also notifies PayAviator's servers when a connection has new or updated transactions, when new accounts are available, or when a connection needs attention, for example when you need to sign in to your bank again, a connection is about to expire, or access was revoked. PayAviator uses these notifications to sync transactions and to show a Reconnect option in the app when a connection needs to be updated.

Before Plaid opens, PayAviator shows a short notice explaining what connecting means, with links to this policy and Plaid's privacy policy. When you tap Continue, the app records on your phone that you agreed, including the time, the policy version, whether you were connecting or reconnecting, and the app version. If you choose Not now, no connection is started.

When you use Refresh to check a live bank balance, we keep a short log of that request (your user ID, the bank connection ID, the time, and whether it succeeded or failed) so we can reconcile billing from our data provider. This log contains no balances, account numbers or bank credentials, and it may be kept after you delete your account.

Banks connected with app versions released before sign-in was added need to be connected again after you sign in. A manual cleanup script exists for connections made before sign-in. This policy does not say those older connections have already been removed.

What's stored where

  • On your phone: bills, income, spending, budgets, reminders, and other information you enter are stored on your phone. If you don't sign in or don't use cloud sync, this information stays on your phone and is not sent to our servers. While you're signed in, the app also keeps a copy of your connected-account and transaction information on your phone. That copy is removed when you sign out, when your sign-in session ends, when you delete your account, or when a different user signs in on the phone.
  • On PayAviator's servers: if you are signed in and use cloud sync, copies of your bills, income, spending, budgets and related settings you enter are stored in PayAviator's database on Railway (api.payaviator.app) and linked to your Firebase user ID, so they can sync between the app and payaviator.app. You can delete them by deleting your account. If you delete one synced item and keep your account, the server source keeps a deletion marker so your other devices can learn it was deleted. A cleanup in that source removes markers older than 90 days. That 90 days is a constant in the server source. Account deletion removes the synced copies themselves, rather than leaving those markers. Connected bank data is also stored so it can sync. For each connected institution this includes the encrypted Plaid access token, the institution's name and identifier, and the connection's status, plus the transactions synced from Plaid and short-lived records used while a connection is being set up. These records are linked to your Firebase user ID. Account details are fetched from Plaid when the app needs them and are not stored on PayAviator's servers. The servers cache only the last known balance for each connected bank, with the time it was updated, so it can be shown quickly. No account numbers or bank credentials are stored (Plaid holds your credentials). This cached balance is deleted when you disconnect a bank or delete your account. The servers also keep the one-way codes described in Preventing misuse of the free bank connection.
  • Device-lock preference: whether the optional app lock is on, and your reminder choices, stay on your phone only (see Optional device lock).
  • With Firebase Authentication: your sign-in account information, described in Account and sign-in.

PayAviator's servers also keep operational logs to troubleshoot problems and protect the service. These logs are limited to technical details such as your Firebase user ID, connection and request identifiers, error codes and messages, and counts. They do not include access tokens, passwords, or your transaction, account, or balance details. Logs are kept by Railway, PayAviator's hosting provider. This policy does not state how many days the host keeps those logs.

Preventing misuse of the free bank connection

PayAviator includes one free connected bank (with all its accounts) per person. To stop the same bank account or phone from being used to claim more than one free connection, PayAviator's servers keep two one-way codes: one made from the bank and the last four digits of the connected account, and one made from an identifier for your phone. These codes are scrambled in a way that can't be turned back into your account number or phone identifier, and PayAviator never stores your full account number.

The codes are used only to check whether a free connection has already been used. They are not used for advertising, are not shared, and are not linked to your transactions. When you disconnect your free bank or delete your account, these codes are kept for a cooldown so the free connection can't be reused right away, and then they are deleted. The server source uses 30 days unless FREE_TIER_COOLDOWN_DAYS is set to another number. This policy does not promise that the production setting is 30 days.

Optional device lock

PayAviator can be locked with your phone's PIN, pattern, password, fingerprint or face scan. This is optional and is off unless you turn it on. After you connect a bank, the app suggests turning it on, with the choices Turn on lock and Not now. If it stays off while a bank is connected, the app may show a gentle reminder when you open it, at most about once every 7 days, with a Don't remind me option. You can turn the lock on or off at any time in Settings.

The lock uses your phone's own screen-lock and biometric system. PayAviator does not receive, see or store your fingerprint, face data, PIN or password. Whether the suggestion has been shown, when it was last shown, and whether you chose Don't remind me are saved only on your phone. They are not sent to PayAviator's servers, and they are cleared if you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Because the lock is optional, a phone that is unlocked or shared can open PayAviator and the bank information it shows while you are signed in; signing out removes bank data from your phone.

Notifications

Bill reminders and custom reminders are scheduled on the phone. They are not sent through Firebase Cloud Messaging. Android can delay or skip them. They do not pay a bill. Turning notifications off on the phone stops them from showing.

Firebase Cloud Messaging is used for bank-reconnect alerts only. The reviewed app ignores a push unless its type is bank reconnect. If you are signed in and allow notifications, the app sends that phone's push token to PayAviator's servers with your Firebase user ID and the device platform. The token is used to deliver those reconnect alerts. When you sign out, the app asks the servers to remove that phone's token. Deleting the account removes the push tokens stored for it. There is no coded retention period for a token after that deletion.

Subscriptions and payments

Your first bank, with all its accounts, is on us. Linking more banks needs PayAviator Plus, shown at $3.99 a month or $29.99 a year. The Android source shows those prices and does not start a Google Play purchase: purchases are switched off in that source, and the app project does not include the Play Billing library. The Play closed test that is active today is 4.2.6, not a production release, and this policy does not say that build can take a Play payment. On payaviator.app, the pricing page opens Stripe Checkout only when the server is configured for Stripe, and otherwise the server refuses it. This policy does not say that production Stripe setting is on. PayAviator does not receive your full card number.

If a subscription is recorded, PayAviator stores your Firebase user ID, the plan, the status and renewal or end date, and a purchase or customer reference. A Play purchase token, if one is ever verified, is stored only as a hash. If web checkout creates a Stripe customer, Stripe receives your email for the receipt, and PayAviator stores the Stripe customer id. Google Play and Stripe keep their own payment records under their own policies. This policy does not say which production keys are set.

How information is used

  • Provide and operate PayAviator features.
  • Display and organize financial information requested by the user.
  • Support budgeting, spending, bill-management, and financial-summary features.
  • Authenticate your PayAviator account and keep connected-account requests tied to your account.
  • Sync connected-account data and keep bank connections working.
  • Maintain and improve reliability and security.
  • Prevent misuse of the free bank connection.
  • Schedule bill reminders on your phone, and send bank-reconnect alerts you allow.
  • Check and manage your Plus subscription.

Sharing

PayAviator does not sell your personal information and does not share it with third parties for their own purposes, including advertising. PayAviator shows no ads and does not use an advertising ID. Information is processed only by the service providers listed below to operate the app, under their own terms and privacy practices, or when required by law. That describes the product. It is not a decision about what a privacy statute means by sell or share. The reviewed app and website source have no advertising sale. The providers below do process information to run PayAviator.

Service providers

PayAviator uses these service providers to operate the app. Each processes information under its own terms and privacy practices.

  • Plaid connects your financial institutions and provides account, balance, and transaction information.
  • Firebase Authentication (Google) provides sign-in and stores your sign-in account information.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) delivers bank-reconnect alerts to a signed-in phone. It is not used for bill reminders in the reviewed app.
  • Google Play Billing is not part of the reviewed Android source, and that source does not start a Play purchase. If a Play subscription exists, Google keeps the payment record. This policy does not say Play billing is active.
  • Stripe is what the website calls for Plus checkout. The server uses it only when Stripe is configured. Production configuration was not read.
  • Railway hosts PayAviator's servers, the database that stores connected bank data, and the servers' operational logs.
  • Cloudflare provides DNS and protection for payaviator.app and api.payaviator.app. Traffic to those addresses passes through Cloudflare, which can see technical request details such as your IP address and the pages requested, and the encrypted content in transit. We do not use it to read your financial data.
  • Zoho Mail provides PayAviator's support email (support@payaviator.app). It sees the messages you send us and our replies, including your email address and anything you choose to include. Please don't email bank passwords or full account numbers.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the payaviator.app website pages. GitHub can see ordinary web request details such as your IP address when you load the site. It does not receive your bills, budgets, or bank data.
  • Google Play Integrity is present in the Android source, but the reviewed build does not send an integrity token because its cloud project number is 0. The server checks a token only if enforcement is turned on. That production switch was not read. This policy does not say that check is running.

User choices

Connecting a financial account is optional. Users may disconnect accounts and may stop using connected-account features. Users should use the available account-management controls for their connected institution or Plaid connection when they want to revoke access.

Signing in is optional unless you want to connect a financial institution. You can sign out at any time. Signing out removes bank data from your phone; the bills and other information you entered stay. Disconnecting one institution connection is separate from deleting your PayAviator account. The server route removes one Plaid Item, which can include several accounts at that institution, and leaves your other connections and your sign-in in place. It does not close the account at the bank. The Android Connected Accounts screen has a Disconnect button on each account row and one confirmation. That button calls the server only when no other account from the same connection remains on the phone. The website does not have this button. See the delete-account page.

Deleting your account

You can delete your PayAviator account in the Android app under Settings > Connected Accounts > Delete account, or on the web under Settings > Delete my account at payaviator.app/app/settings#delete-account. You can also request deletion by email as described on the Delete your account page. For your security, if you haven't signed in recently, you'll be asked to sign in again before deletion starts. Deleting your account asks PayAviator to remove every bank connection at Plaid and then delete the bank connections, synced transactions, pending connection requests, connection-token records, and push tokens stored for your account. If you use cloud sync, the copies of your bills, income, spending, cushion and budget stored on PayAviator's servers are deleted with your account too, whether you started from the app or the website. The one-way codes are released into the cooldown described under Preventing misuse of the free bank connection. They are not deleted at the same moment as the bank rows, and this policy does not state a production number of days. After the server deletion succeeds, your sign-in account is deleted from Firebase Authentication. If the server reports that a bank could not be removed, the sign-in account is not deleted.

If Plaid can't remove one of your bank connections, that bank's connection and transactions stay on PayAviator's servers so the removal can be tried again, and your sign-in account is not deleted yet. Banks that were removed stay removed. The app or the website tells you the deletion didn't finish, and you can try again.

Deleting your account does not cancel a PayAviator Plus subscription. Cancel Plus separately in Google Play, or, if you subscribed on payaviator.app, from your plan page or by emailing support@payaviator.app. Uninstalling the app does not cancel Plus and does not delete data stored on PayAviator's servers.

The account-deletion request does not remove every server record. The reviewed code keeps these, and it does not set an end date for them. The product does not set an end date for them. This policy does not add one.

  • The balance-refresh log (user id, bank connection id, time, outcome, and error code). It has no balances or account numbers. No purge of that table was found.
  • The subscription status row: product id, platform, state, end time, and, for Play, a hash of a purchase token rather than the raw token.
  • The Stripe customer id stored with the Firebase user id, if web checkout created one.
  • Stripe event ids used so the same notice is not applied twice. The source includes a purge. Its production timing was not confirmed.
  • Host log lines. The host's retention period was not read.

PayAviator does not receive your full card number. Google Play and Stripe keep their own payment records under their policies.

Bills, income, spending, budgets, reminders, and other information you entered stay on your phone after you delete your account. Signing out clears the phone's bank cache. It does not clear those entered items. You can delete them in the app or by uninstalling the app. Uninstalling does not delete a backup or export you saved yourself. Delete that file separately if you no longer want it.

Website and this browser

This policy describes PayAviator at payaviator.app and api.payaviator.app. The website pages do not include an advertising or analytics script, and they do not set an analytics cookie of their own. When you sign in, Firebase Authentication keeps the sign-in session in this browser. While you are signed in, the web app keeps the working copy of synced bills, income, spending and budgets in memory for that visit. It also saves a few choices in this browser, such as which accounts you include in Available Now, cash you enter on the web, and which month you are viewing. Signing out clears that in-memory copy. Choices saved in the browser can remain until you clear this site's data. Bank lists shown on the web are read from PayAviator's servers for that visit; connecting or disconnecting a bank is done in the Android app.

Security

All data sent between the app, payaviator.app, and PayAviator's servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Every request for connected-account data is authenticated with your Firebase ID token, and PayAviator's servers return only the data linked to your account. Plaid access tokens are encrypted at rest (AES-256) and are never sent to your phone. Notifications from Plaid are accepted only when Plaid's signature is valid.

You can add an optional device lock (see Optional device lock) for extra protection on your phone. PayAviator uses reasonable safeguards intended to protect information. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Age requirement

PayAviator is intended for adults 18 and older. The app does not ask your age and has no automatic process that finds and deletes an under-18 account. If you believe a child has given us information, email support@payaviator.app.
