PayAviator Privacy Policy

PayAviator is a personal finance application designed to help users organize bills, budgets, spending, income, debts, and connected financial-account information.

Information you provide

PayAviator may process information you enter into the app, such as bills, income, spending, budgets, debt information, savings settings, notes, reminders, and other information used to provide app features.

Account and sign-in

You can use PayAviator's bill, income, spending, and budgeting features without a PayAviator account. An account is needed only to connect a financial institution and sync connected-account data.

PayAviator accounts use Firebase Authentication, a Google service. You can sign in with your Google account or with an email address and password. Firebase Authentication stores a unique user ID for your account, your email address, and basic sign-in records such as when the account was created and when you last signed in. If you sign in with Google, Firebase also receives your Google account display name and a link to your profile photo. If you use an email address and password, your password is handled by Firebase Authentication; PayAviator's servers never receive or store it.

PayAviator's servers identify you by your Firebase user ID only. They do not store your email address, name, or photo.

Connected financial accounts

If you choose to connect a financial institution, PayAviator uses Plaid to facilitate that connection. Depending on the features you authorize and the data made available by your institution, PayAviator may receive account information, balances, and transaction information. This information is used to provide user-requested personal-finance features such as displaying account activity, supporting budgeting, and helping calculate financial summaries.

PayAviator does not require you to give your online-banking password directly to PayAviator. Authentication with supported institutions is handled through the Plaid connection experience.

Connecting a financial institution requires a PayAviator account. When you connect, Plaid provides PayAviator's servers with an access token for that connection. Access tokens are encrypted on PayAviator's servers (AES-256) and are never sent to your phone.

Plaid also notifies PayAviator's servers when a connection has new or updated transactions, when new accounts are available, or when a connection needs attention, for example when you need to sign in to your bank again, a connection is about to expire, or access was revoked. PayAviator uses these notifications to sync transactions and to show a Reconnect option in the app when a connection needs to be updated.

Before Plaid opens, PayAviator shows a short notice explaining what connecting means, with links to this policy and Plaid's privacy policy. When you tap Continue, the app records on your phone that you agreed, including the time, the policy version, whether you were connecting or reconnecting, and the app version. If you choose Not now, no connection is started.

Banks connected with app versions released before sign-in was added need to be connected again after you sign in. After the sign-in update is released, PayAviator will remove those earlier bank connections at Plaid and delete their stored data.

What's stored where

  • On your phone: bills, income, spending, budgets, reminders, and other information you enter are stored on your phone. While you're signed in, the app also keeps a copy of your connected-account and transaction information on your phone. That copy is removed when you sign out, when your sign-in session ends, when you delete your account, or when a different user signs in on the phone.
  • On PayAviator's servers: connected bank data is stored so it can sync. For each connected institution this includes the encrypted Plaid access token, the institution's name and identifier, and the connection's status, plus the transactions synced from Plaid and short-lived records used while a connection is being set up. These records are linked to your Firebase user ID. Account details and balances are not stored on PayAviator's servers; they are fetched live from Plaid when the app needs them.
  • With Firebase Authentication: your sign-in account information, described in Account and sign-in.

PayAviator's servers also keep operational logs to troubleshoot problems and protect the service. These logs are limited to technical details such as your Firebase user ID, connection and request identifiers, error codes and messages, and counts. They do not include access tokens, passwords, or your transaction, account, or balance details. Logs are kept by Railway, PayAviator's hosting provider, for the period set by its plan.

How information is used

  • Provide and operate PayAviator features.
  • Display and organize financial information requested by the user.
  • Support budgeting, spending, bill-management, and financial-summary features.
  • Authenticate your PayAviator account and keep connected-account requests tied to your account.
  • Sync connected-account data and keep bank connections working.
  • Maintain and improve reliability and security.

Sharing

PayAviator does not sell users' personal financial information. Information may be processed by service providers used to operate app functionality, such as Plaid for connected financial accounts, subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

Service providers

PayAviator uses these service providers to operate the app. Each processes information under its own terms and privacy practices.

  • Plaid connects your financial institutions and provides account, balance, and transaction information.
  • Firebase Authentication (Google) provides sign-in and stores your sign-in account information.
  • Railway hosts PayAviator's servers, the database that stores connected bank data, and the servers' operational logs.

User choices

Connecting a financial account is optional. Users may disconnect accounts and may stop using connected-account features. Users should use the available account-management controls for their connected institution or Plaid connection when they want to revoke access.

Signing in is optional unless you want to connect a financial institution. You can sign out at any time. Signing out removes bank data from your phone; the bills and other information you entered stay. Disconnecting a bank in the app removes that connection at Plaid and deletes its transactions stored on PayAviator's servers.

Deleting your account

You can delete your PayAviator account in the app from Connected Accounts. For your security, if you haven't signed in recently, you'll be asked to sign in again first. Deleting your account removes every bank connection at Plaid and then deletes all of your data stored on PayAviator's servers: your bank connections, synced transactions, pending connection requests, and connection-token records. After that, your sign-in account is deleted from Firebase Authentication.

If Plaid can't remove one of your bank connections, that bank's connection and transactions stay on PayAviator's servers so the removal can be tried again, and your sign-in account is not deleted yet. Banks that were removed stay removed. The app tells you the deletion didn't finish, and you can try again.

Bills, income, spending, and other information you added stay on your phone after you delete your account. You can delete that information in the app or by uninstalling the app. Uninstalling doesn't delete backup or export files you saved yourself, wherever you chose to save them; delete those separately if you no longer want them.

Security

Connections between the app and PayAviator's servers use HTTPS. Every request for connected-account data is authenticated with your Firebase ID token, and PayAviator's servers return only the data linked to your account. Plaid access tokens are encrypted at rest (AES-256) and are never sent to your phone. Notifications from Plaid are accepted only when Plaid's signature is valid.

PayAviator uses reasonable safeguards intended to protect information. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children

PayAviator is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

This policy may be updated as PayAviator changes. The effective date above will be updated when material revisions are published.

Contact

Privacy or support questions can be sent to support@payaviator.app.
