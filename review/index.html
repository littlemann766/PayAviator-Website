What PayAviator does

These pages are privacy version 2026-11a, effective November 9, 2026. They are not a compliance certification and not legal advice. Sample screens are demo pictures. Nothing on this map signs in, cancels, deletes, or connects a bank.

Today

PayAviator lets a person enter bills, income, spending, and budgets, and see estimates such as Safe to Spend. Those figures are not a bank balance and not financial, tax, credit, or legal advice.

Connecting a bank is optional and uses Plaid. PayAviator does not ask for the bank password on its own page. One connection is one institution link and can include several accounts, such as checking and savings. The first connection is included. Plus is shown at $3.99 a month or $29.99 a year for more banks. The Android source shows those prices and does not start a Google Play purchase. The website opens Stripe Checkout only when the server is configured. This page does not say that production setting is on.

Sign-in is Google or email through Firebase. The contact address is support@payaviator.app.

Installed apps during Play review

Play closed testing still has Alpha 4.2.6, version code 420600, active. That build stores privacy 2026-10g. Production is not eligible: 8 testers are on the test, and production needs 12 testers for 14 days. This site does not put the app on the production track.

While Google reviews a newer Android App Bundle, 4.2.6 remains the build testers already have. Opening the new privacy page does not change the version stored on those phones. The old text stays at privacy version 2026-10g. The old terms stay at terms dated October 15, 2026. A phone records 2026-11a only after an update that contains that version is installed and the person continues a bank connection under it.

Account, cancel, and disconnect

Manage or cancel reads the signed-in account status. A Stripe subscription asks the server to open that user’s Stripe portal. A Google Play subscription opens Google’s subscription page for package com.payaviator.app, using the product id on the status when there is one and it is not a Stripe price id. Opening that page does not mean the subscription is already cancelled. If the server has no end date, the page does not invent one. This map does not say the Stripe portal is configured or that it shows a cancel control.

Deleting the PayAviator account does not cancel the provider subscription and does not close the accounts at the bank. If one bank connection cannot be removed, the sign-in stays and the page does not say the account was deleted. Some billing records can remain. This page does not set a retention period the product does not implement.

Disconnecting one institution connection leaves the other connections and the PayAviator account in place. The website does not have a disconnect button. The Android screen does. A change that would unlink one account inside a connection, while leaving the others, is not what the reviewed source does.

Five questions

These are questions. They are not legal claims, and this site does not add a liability cap, a court venue, or an immunity.

  1. How long should the balance-refresh log, the subscription-status row, and a Stripe customer id be kept after account deletion? The product does not set an end date for them.
  2. The app does not ask age. Should the 18-and-older rule be enforced in the product?
  3. Does the product statement that PayAviator does not sell personal information, and does not share it for advertising, match what an applicable privacy statute means by sell or share?
  4. The owner is forming an Ohio LLC alone. It is not registered. What, if anything, should the policy say after registration? This site does not say a company is already formed, and it does not name another owner.
  5. If some bank connections are removed and sign-in deletion then fails, those connections stay removed. Is any further notice required?

Not confirmed in production

  • The free-connection cooldown in the server source defaults to 30 days. The production setting was not read, so 30 days is not a promise.
  • Whether Stripe is configured, and whether its portal shows cancel, was not read.
  • A Google Play product id is used only when account status returns one. This page does not treat an example id as the store product.
