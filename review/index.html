What PayAviator does

This page is draft 2026-11a-draft. It is not the live policy. The live privacy policy is version 2026-10g, effective October 17, 2026, at payaviator.app/privacy.html. This draft is not binding, not a compliance claim, and not legal advice.

Today

PayAviator lets a person enter bills, income, spending, and budgets, and see estimates such as Safe to Spend. Those figures are not a bank balance and not financial, tax, credit, or legal advice.

Connecting a bank is optional and uses Plaid. PayAviator does not ask for the bank password on its own page. One connection is one institution link and can include several accounts, such as checking and savings. The first connection is included. The pricing page shows Plus at $3.99 a month or $29.99 a year for more banks. The reviewed Android source shows those prices and does not start a Google Play purchase. The website opens Stripe Checkout only when the server is configured. That production setting was not read for this draft.

Sign-in is Google or email through Firebase. It is needed to connect a bank and to sync. The contact address already used in the product is support@payaviator.app.

Pages to open

  • Privacy draft — what is stored, who processes it, and what deletion does not erase.
  • Terms draft — estimates, subscriptions, and the support aim.
  • Support — independently operated; aim to respond within 72 hours; urgent fraud goes to the bank.
  • Delete account — one confirmation. Does not cancel Plus or close the bank account.
  • Pricing — $3.99 a month or $29.99 a year.
  • How it works — Safe to Spend and Plaid, with the existing estimate disclaimer.
  • Signed-in plan — Manage or cancel, after sign-in. Stripe or Google Play is chosen from the account status, not from a typed customer id.
  • Signed-in delete — one Delete account button, with Manage or cancel beside it. It does not ask why.

Account and subscription

Manage or cancel reads the signed-in account status. A Stripe subscription asks the server to open that user’s Stripe portal. A Google Play subscription opens Google’s subscription page for package com.payaviator.app, using the product id on the status when there is one. Opening that page does not mean the subscription is already cancelled. If the server has no end date, the page does not invent one.

Deleting the PayAviator account does not cancel the provider subscription and does not close the accounts at the bank. If one bank connection cannot be removed, the sign-in stays and the page does not say the account was deleted. Some billing records can remain. How long they are kept is not decided. This draft does not promise that everything is erased at once.

Disconnecting one institution connection leaves the other connections and the PayAviator account in place. The website does not have a disconnect button. The Android screen does. A change that would unlink one account inside a connection, while leaving the others, is not what the reviewed source does.

Not decided

  • How long the balance-refresh log, subscription-status row, and Stripe customer id stay after account deletion.
  • Whether the free-connection cooldown in production is the source default of 30 days.
  • Whether Stripe is configured in production, and whether the Stripe portal includes a cancel control.
  • Which Google Play product id is on the installed app. This draft does not treat a product id as confirmed unless the account status returns it.
  • The app does not ask the person’s age. Whether to enforce 18 is open.
  • What “sell” or “share” means under a privacy statute. This draft does not decide that.
  • The owner is forming the business alone. This draft does not say a company is already formed, and it does not name another owner.
  • If some bank connections are removed and the sign-in deletion then fails, those connections are not put back.

Publishing this draft means printing version 2026-11a, removing these draft notices, and setting the Android app’s POLICY_VERSION to 2026-11a in the same release. Until then the app stays on 2026-10g.
