Account

Delete your PayAviator account

Unpublished draft

Prepared November 7, 2026 with privacy draft 2026-11a-draft. This page is not effective and is not deployed. The live delete-account page stays as published until this draft is published with privacy version 2026-11a and the Android app's POLICY_VERSION is set to 2026-11a in the same release.

You can ask to delete your PayAviator account. What the reviewed code actually removes is described below. This draft does not promise a 30-day completion time.

In short

  • Disconnecting one bank connection is not the same as deleting your PayAviator account, and it does not close the account at your bank.
  • Delete account, in the Android app: Settings > Connected Accounts > Delete account. One confirmation. No reason and no survey. You do not have to cancel Plus first.
  • Delete account, on the web: payaviator.app/app/settings#delete-account. One confirmation: type DELETE. No reason and no survey. If you have not signed in recently, you sign in again before anything is deleted.
  • The website does not have a button that disconnects one bank. That control is in the Android app, and the server removes a whole institution connection, not one account inside it. See Disconnect one connection.
  • Email to support@payaviator.app is not an automatic deletion route. No coded deadline was found.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel Plus. Plus can keep renewing until you cancel it on its own.
  • Some records described in the Privacy Policy are not erased immediately.

How to request deletion

Email support@payaviator.app from the email address you use to sign in to PayAviator, with the subject Delete my account. We use your account email to confirm the request comes from you.

Delete it myself (sign in) Email a deletion request

What is deleted

Delete account is one confirmation. The Android app asks you to confirm once. The website asks you to type DELETE. It does not ask why, and it does not make you cancel Plus before it continues. If the sign-in is not recent, you are asked to sign in again and nothing is deleted yet. Then PayAviator asks the server to remove each institution connection at Plaid. The sign-in account is deleted only if that removal succeeds. An email is not this route. When the in-app or web deletion succeeds, it:

  • Removes your linked bank connections at Plaid and deletes their synced transactions from PayAviator's servers, when Plaid accepts the removal.
  • Deletes pending connection requests, connection-token records, and push notification tokens stored for your account.
  • If you use cloud sync, deletes the synced copies of your bills, income, spending, cushion and budget on PayAviator's servers.
  • Then deletes your PayAviator sign-in account from Firebase Authentication. Firebase holds the email address used to sign in; PayAviator's own servers do not store that email.

Information stored only on your phone stays there until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. If Plaid cannot remove a bank, that bank's connection stays so the removal can be tried again, and the sign-in account is not deleted yet.

What may be kept

Account deletion does not erase every record, and it does not cancel Plus. The reviewed code keeps the items below and does not set an end date for them. How long they should be kept is an owner and counsel question. This draft does not invent a period. Details are in the Privacy Policy draft.

  • One-way codes for the free bank connection stay for a cooldown, then are deleted. The source default is 30 days unless the server setting is different. The production setting was not read, so 30 days is not a promise.
  • The balance-refresh log (user id, bank connection id, time, and success or failure, with no balances or account numbers). No purge of that log was found.
  • The subscription status row, and the Stripe customer id if web checkout created one. PayAviator does not receive your full card number.
  • Google Play and Stripe keep their own payment records under their policies.
  • Phone-entered bills, income, spending, budgets, reminders, and any backup or export you saved yourself.
  • If you delete one synced item and keep the account, the server source keeps a deletion marker and removes markers older than 90 days. That number is a constant in the source.

Delete your data but keep your account

On the web, Settings > Delete synced data is a third action. It removes synced bills, income, spending and budget from PayAviator's servers. It leaves your PayAviator account and your bank connections in place. It does not disconnect a bank. Data on your phone stays until you clear it there. There is no website button that disconnects one bank. Linked banks are read-only on the web.

Disconnect one connection

This is not Delete account, and it does not close the checking, savings, or card account at your bank. The reviewed server has one disconnect route, DELETE /api/plaid/items/:itemId. It removes that one Plaid Item: the institution connection, which can include more than one account, such as checking and savings at the same bank. Other connections stay. Your PayAviator sign-in stays. Bills, income, and budget stored for the account stay. Plus is not cancelled. If Plaid cannot remove that connection, the server keeps it so you can try again.

In the Android app, Settings > Connected Accounts shows Disconnect on each account row. The confirmation is one dialog, "Disconnect account?", with no reason and no survey. The reviewed code removes that row from the phone immediately. It calls the server route above only when no other account from the same connection remains on the phone. If other accounts from that connection remain, this draft does not claim the server unlinks the one account you tapped. The app review is still tracing that gap. There is no separate server route for one account inside a connection.

A credit-card Disconnect control in the reviewed app removes that card from the phone list only. It does not call the server route above. The website has no disconnect button.

More details are in the Privacy Policy.
