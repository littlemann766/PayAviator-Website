Account

Delete your PayAviator account

You can ask us to delete your PayAviator account and its data at any time.

In short

  • Email support@payaviator.app from your account email with the subject "Delete my account".
  • We delete your account and data within 30 days.
  • You can also delete just your data, or disconnect a bank in the app.

Tap a heading below to read the full text.

How to request deletion

Email support@payaviator.app from the email address you use to sign in to PayAviator, with the subject Delete my account. We use your account email to confirm the request comes from you.

Email a deletion request

What is deleted

Within 30 days of your request we delete:

  • Your PayAviator sign-in account and email address.
  • Your linked bank connections. They are removed at Plaid, and synced transactions are deleted from PayAviator's servers.
  • Push notification tokens for your devices.

Information stored only on your phone stays there until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.

What may be kept

We keep only what the law requires. For example, payment and subscription records are held by Google Play or Stripe under their own policies.

Delete your data but keep your account

If you want to delete your data without closing your account, email support@payaviator.app from your account email and tell us what you'd like removed.

Disconnect a bank at any time

You can disconnect a linked bank at any time in the app under Settings > Connected Accounts. This removes the connection at Plaid and deletes its transactions from PayAviator's servers.

More details are in the Privacy Policy.
