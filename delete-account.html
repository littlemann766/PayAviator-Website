Account

Delete your PayAviator account

Unpublished draft

Prepared November 7, 2026 with privacy draft 2026-11a-draft. This page is not effective and is not deployed. The live delete-account page stays as published until this draft is published with privacy version 2026-11a and the Android app's POLICY_VERSION is set to 2026-11a in the same release.

You can ask to delete your PayAviator account. What the reviewed code actually removes is described below. This draft does not promise a 30-day completion time.

In short

  • In the Android app: Settings > Connected Accounts > Delete account.
  • On the web: sign in and open payaviator.app/app/settings#delete-account (Settings > Delete my account). If you have not signed in recently, you will be asked to sign in again before deletion starts.
  • Or email support@payaviator.app from your account email with the subject "Delete my account". No email-deletion job or coded deadline was found. How soon an email request is completed is an owner question. This draft does not promise 30 days.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel Plus. Cancel the subscription separately.
  • Some records described in the Privacy Policy are not erased immediately.

How to request deletion

Email support@payaviator.app from the email address you use to sign in to PayAviator, with the subject Delete my account. We use your account email to confirm the request comes from you.

Delete it myself (sign in) Email a deletion request

What is deleted

When you confirm Delete account in the Android app or on the web, and the sign-in is recent, PayAviator asks the server to remove each bank at Plaid. The sign-in account is deleted only if that removal succeeds. An email request has no coded deadline. This draft does not promise that an email is completed within 30 days. When the in-app or web deletion succeeds, it:

  • Removes your linked bank connections at Plaid and deletes their synced transactions from PayAviator's servers, when Plaid accepts the removal.
  • Deletes pending connection requests, connection-token records, and push notification tokens stored for your account.
  • If you use cloud sync, deletes the synced copies of your bills, income, spending, cushion and budget on PayAviator's servers.
  • Then deletes your PayAviator sign-in account from Firebase Authentication. Firebase holds the email address used to sign in; PayAviator's own servers do not store that email.

Information stored only on your phone stays there until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. If Plaid cannot remove a bank, that bank's connection stays so the removal can be tried again, and the sign-in account is not deleted yet.

What may be kept

Account deletion does not erase every record, and it does not cancel Plus. The reviewed code keeps the items below and does not set an end date for them. How long they should be kept is an owner and counsel question. This draft does not invent a period. Details are in the Privacy Policy draft.

  • One-way codes for the free bank connection stay for a cooldown, then are deleted. The source default is 30 days unless the server setting is different. The production setting was not read, so 30 days is not a promise.
  • The balance-refresh log (user id, bank connection id, time, and success or failure, with no balances or account numbers). No purge of that log was found.
  • The subscription status row, and the Stripe customer id if web checkout created one. PayAviator does not receive your full card number.
  • Google Play and Stripe keep their own payment records under their policies.
  • Phone-entered bills, income, spending, budgets, reminders, and any backup or export you saved yourself.
  • If you delete one synced item and keep the account, the server source keeps a deletion marker and removes markers older than 90 days. That number is a constant in the source.

Delete your data but keep your account

On the web, Settings > Delete synced data removes synced bills, income, spending and budget from PayAviator's servers and leaves your account and bank connections in place. Data on your phone stays until you clear it there. To disconnect a bank without deleting your account, use the Android app under Settings > Connected Accounts. The web shows linked banks as read-only. For any other removal, email support@payaviator.app from your account email and tell us what you'd like removed.

Disconnect a bank at any time

You can disconnect a linked bank at any time in the app under Settings > Connected Accounts. This removes the connection at Plaid and deletes its transactions from PayAviator's servers.

More details are in the Privacy Policy.
