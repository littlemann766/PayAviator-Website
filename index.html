Connected financial accounts

Pay-Pilot uses Plaid to let users connect supported financial institutions. When a user chooses to connect an account, Pay-Pilot may access account and balance information and transaction information made available through Plaid so the app can display financial activity and support budgeting and money-management features.

Pay-Pilot does not ask users to provide their online-banking password directly to Pay-Pilot. Account connections are initiated through Plaid. Users control whether they connect an account and can disconnect connected accounts.

For more information, read the Pay-Pilot Privacy Policy.
