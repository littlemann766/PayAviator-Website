Device security

Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old to use PayAviator. The reviewed app source does not ask your age or block an account for age. Whether to enforce that rule is an owner and counsel question. This draft does not add an age gate.

Subscriptions

Your first bank, with all its accounts, is on us. Linking more banks needs a PayAviator Plus subscription: $3.99 a month or $29.99 a year (save 37% compared with paying monthly). Prices are in US dollars and may not include taxes. The Android source reviewed on November 7, 2026 shows those prices and does not start a Google Play purchase. A store binary was not checked. This website is built to open Stripe Checkout, and the server completes it only when Stripe is configured. Production Stripe settings were not read, so these terms do not say web checkout is confirmed on. If a Plus subscription is active, the renewal and cancellation rules below apply.

Automatic renewal. If Plus is purchased, it renews automatically at the end of each monthly or yearly period, and you are charged the then-current price, until you cancel. We will tell you before any price change takes effect.

Cancelling. The reviewed Android source has no in-app control that starts or cancels a Google Play purchase. If a subscription was bought through Google Play, cancel it in the Google Play Store app under Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions. If you subscribed on payaviator.app, cancel from payaviator.app/app/settings#plan (Manage subscription). payaviator.app/account is a short page that links there. You can also email support@payaviator.app. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription. Deleting your PayAviator account does not cancel Plus either; cancel first or the plan can keep renewing. After you cancel, Plus stays active until the end of the period you already paid for.

Refunds. Refunds for purchases made through Google Play follow Google Play's refund policy. For purchases made on payaviator.app through Stripe, ask for a refund by emailing support@payaviator.app. Except where the law requires otherwise, payments are not refunded for partial periods.

If your subscription ends, your first bank keeps syncing. Any other banks you linked are paused: they stop syncing until you subscribe again, and you can disconnect them anytime.

Contact

Questions about these terms can be sent to support@payaviator.app. PayAviator is independently operated. Support is not monitored continuously. We aim to respond to support requests within 72 hours. That aim is a first response, counted in elapsed hours, not a guarantee that the issue will be resolved in 72 hours. Some issues require additional investigation and may take longer to resolve. We do not send an automatic reply. The aim does not delay bank disconnection or account deletion that you start yourself, and it does not change a deadline imposed by law. For urgent banking issues or suspected fraud, contact your bank directly.
