Subscriptions

Your first bank account is on us. Linking more accounts needs a PayAviator Plus subscription: $3.99 a month or $29.99 a year (save 37% compared with paying monthly). Prices are in US dollars and may not include taxes. You can subscribe in the Android app through Google Play or on payaviator.app through Stripe.

Automatic renewal. Plus renews automatically at the end of each monthly or yearly period, and you are charged the then-current price, until you cancel. We will tell you before any price change takes effect.

Cancelling. If you subscribed in the app, cancel in the Google Play Store app under Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions. If you subscribed on payaviator.app, cancel from your account page at payaviator.app/account (Manage subscription) or by emailing support@payaviator.app. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription. After you cancel, Plus stays active until the end of the period you already paid for.

Refunds. Refunds for purchases made through Google Play follow Google Play's refund policy. For purchases made on payaviator.app through Stripe, ask for a refund by emailing support@payaviator.app. Except where the law requires otherwise, payments are not refunded for partial periods.

If your subscription ends, accounts you already linked stay linked, but you can't link another account beyond the first until you subscribe again.
